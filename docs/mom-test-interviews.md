# Mom Test interview script (homeowners)

Use past behavior only. Do not pitch SolarFlow. Validate traffic ideas by counting repeated **behaviors**, not compliments.

## Recruiting filter

Talk to people who **looked at solar in the last 12 months** (searched, got a quote, or talked to an installer) — not “we might someday.”

## Opening

- “Walk me through the last time you looked at solar. Where did you start?”
- “What did you Google / ask ChatGPT / ask a neighbor?”

## Decision stalls

- “Did you get a quote? What happened next?”
- “Where did you get stuck or give up?”
- “What did you already pay for (audit, consultation, aggregator, etc.)?”

## Social proof / distribution

- “Who else have you talked to about this (spouse, Reddit, Nextdoor, coworker)?”
- “Did you ever forward numbers or a PDF to someone? What was missing?”

## Utility / bill

- “Which utility is on your bill? Did a specific bill month trigger research?”
- “What rate schedule are you on, if you know?”

## Avoid

- “Would you use a solar ROI calculator?”
- “Isn’t this cool?”
- Leading questions about our product features

## Scoring

After 5–10 interviews, tally frequency × money/time wasted for:

1. Distrust of savings numbers / quote paralysis  
2. Permit / HOA / process surprise  
3. Roof feasibility uncertainty  
4. Battery / backup vs bill savings confusion  
5. Spouse / joint decision friction  

Ship acquisition surfaces against the top pains only.
